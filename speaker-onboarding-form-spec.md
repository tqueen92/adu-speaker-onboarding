# ADU Speaker Onboarding — Google Form Spec

Copy-paste this to create the form in 5 minutes. Share the form link with speakers,
and share THIS doc with teammates so anyone can recreate it for a new event.

---

## 1. Setup

1. Go to forms.google.com → Blank form.
2. Click the **palette icon** (Customize theme) → set:
   - **Theme color:** `#D81B60` (ADU hot pink)
   - **Header:** upload the ADU banner image (pink gradient) OR leave blank for a clean pink header
3. **Title:** `Amsterdam Data Union — Speaker Onboarding`
4. **Description:** paste the welcome text below.

---

## 2. Form description (top)

> Welcome to Amsterdam Data Union! We're so glad to have you at our event.
>
> To get everything ready, we need two quick things from you: a short bio and a photo. This takes about two minutes.
>
> **Your bio** — 2–3 sentences, third person, max 200 words. What you do, and what you'll share at the event.
>
> **Example:**
> *"Alexander Modestov is Head of Analytics at Manychat, where he leads the analytics function and has spent the last year building their in-house agentic setup. He's sharing what they built, why they chose to build over buy, and where it still falls short."*

---

## 3. Questions (in this order)

| # | Type | Question text | Settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short answer | **Your name** (as it should appear publicly) | Required |
| 2 | Short answer | **Company & role** | Required |
| 3 | Paragraph | **Your bio** | Required · add "Response validation → Maximum character count → 1200" (≈200 words) |
| 4 | File upload | **Your photo** (square, high-res, just your face) | Required · allow image files only · max 1 file |

---

## 4. Confirmation message (the "Thank you! Next steps")

Under **Settings → Presentation → Confirmation message**, paste:

> Thank you! 🎉 Here's what happens next:
>
> 1. You'll receive our template panel questions.
> 2. We'll schedule a 1-hour call with you and the moderator before the event to review your answers.
> 3. Show up on the day — bring the good vibes, we'll handle the rest.
>
> See you there ✦

---

## 5. Responses go to Drive (automatic)

When the first speaker submits, Google Forms creates a **Responses** spreadsheet.
Click the green **"Link to Sheets"** icon → the responses (name, role, bio, photo link) land in a Sheet in your Drive.

To reuse for the next event: **File → Make a copy** in the form, change the title/date, and it's ready.

---

## Quick reference for teammates

- **New event:** File → Make a copy → update title + date + speaker example.
- **Share with speaker:** the form's "Send" link.
- **See responses:** the linked Sheet.
- **Download photos:** click each photo link in the Sheet column.
