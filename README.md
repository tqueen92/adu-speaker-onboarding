# ADU Speaker & Moderator Onboarding

A one-page welcome guide for speakers and moderators at Amsterdam Data Union (ADU) events. Visitors pick their role and see what to expect before, during and after the event.

**Live page:** https://tqueen92.github.io/adu-speaker-onboarding/

## Links to share

- Speakers: https://tqueen92.github.io/adu-speaker-onboarding/#speaker
- Moderators: https://tqueen92.github.io/adu-speaker-onboarding/#moderator

These links open the page with the right role already selected.

## How to update the text

Everything is in `index.html`. No build step needed.

1. Open `index.html` on GitHub and click the ✏️ pencil icon.
2. Find the text you want to change. The steps are grouped by role:
   - Speaker steps are under `data-for="speaker"`
   - Moderator steps are under `data-for="moderator"`
3. Click **Commit changes**.

GitHub Pages publishes the change in 1 to 5 minutes. Use **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac) or **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) to reload without the old cached version.
