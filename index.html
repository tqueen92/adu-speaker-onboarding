About ADU

Amsterdam Data Union is a curated community of medior and senior data professionals in the Netherlands. We host in-person events where practitioners share what actually works: real implementations, honest lessons, no product pitches.

↓

Why we introduce you

Our events are only as good as the people who make them: the speakers and the moderator. That's why we introduce you to the community, so people know who you are, what you do, and why your perspective matters.

Share the event on LinkedIn and tag us. We'll repost it so more people see it.

↓

How it works

  1. Fill in the form with a short bio and a photo. We use them for the event announcement.
  2. We'll schedule one-hour prep calls to walk through the panel questions. Speakers join one call. The moderator joins every speaker's call.
  3. On the day, speakers share their story, the moderator guides the conversation, and we finish with an open Q&A.
↓

What we share

Before we post about what you said, we check it with you.
