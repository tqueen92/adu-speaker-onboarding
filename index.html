About ADU

Amsterdam Data Union is a curated community of medior and senior data professionals in the Netherlands. We host in-person events where practitioners share what actually works: real implementations, honest lessons, no product pitches.

↓

You are important ⭐️

Our events are only as good as the people who make them: the speakers and the moderator. That's why we introduce you to the community, so people know who you are, what you do, and why your perspective matters.

↓

How it works

Before the event

  1. Fill in the form with a short bio and a photo.
  2. Share the event to your network.
  3. We'll schedule one-hour prep calls to walk through the panel questions. Speakers join one call. The moderator joins every speaker's call.

During the event

  1. Be at the venue for the panel discussion. No video recordings.

After the event

  1. After checking with you, we publish the key takeaways from the discussion with the community.
