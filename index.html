About ADU

Amsterdam Data Union (ADU) is a trusted network of data people in the Netherlands. We host panels and talks where companies share openly. No sales pitch. Small room, face to face.

↓

How it works

For speakers.

Before the event⌄
  1. We introduce you to our community on LinkedIn. We might ask you for a short bio and a photo for that.
  2. Share the event with your network. Your people are very welcome!
  3. We'll send you the panel questions early, so you have plenty of time to think them over.
  4. About 1–2 weeks before, we'll have a relaxed one-hour prep call with you, the moderator and one of us. We walk through your answers so you feel comfortable on the night.
On the day⌄
  1. Come by at 18:00 so we can catch up and align on the topic before things start.
  2. Then it's panel time. No video recordings, so you can speak freely.
  3. Stay for drinks after the panel. It all happens in one evening.
After the event⌄
  1. We write up the key takeaways for the community. You'll read it first, and we only publish after checking with you.

For moderators.

Before the event⌄
  1. We introduce you to our community on LinkedIn. We might ask you for a short bio and a photo for that.
  2. Share the event with your network. Your people are very welcome!
  3. You shape the panel questions, and one of us is happy to brainstorm with you. We send them to the speakers early so they have time to prepare.
  4. About 1–2 weeks before, you join a one-hour call with each speaker separately. It's just you, the speaker and one of us. You go through their answers together and share feedback, so everyone feels ready.
On the day⌄
  1. Come by at 18:00 so we can catch up and align on the topic before things start.
  2. Then you lead the panel. No video recordings, so everyone can speak freely.
  3. Stay for drinks after the panel. It all happens in one evening.
